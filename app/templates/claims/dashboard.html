NHIS Claims Processing

This page shows all NHIS (National Health Insurance Scheme) claims. These claims cannot be paid directly by patients. They must be processed through the NHIS claims system:

  1. Export claims to CSV or Excel format using the export buttons
  2. Submit the exported file to NHIA (National Health Insurance Authority)
  3. Update claim status as you receive confirmations from NHIA
