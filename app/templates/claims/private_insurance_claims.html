Private Insurance Claims Processing

This page shows all invoices for Private Insurance patients. These invoices cannot be paid directly by patients. They must be processed through the claims system:

  1. Export invoices to CSV or Excel format using the export buttons
  2. Submit the exported file to the insurance provider
  3. Update invoice status as you receive payment confirmations from the provider
