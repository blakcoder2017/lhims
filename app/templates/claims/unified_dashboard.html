Local Claims Tracking

This dashboard tracks all insurance claims locally since the system is not connected to external insurance APIs. Claims are organized by provider with submission status badges:

  • Provider Badges: Identify NHIS vs Private Insurance providers
  • Submission Status: Track what has been submitted to each provider
  • Update Status: Click "Update Status" button on any claim to change its status (Draft → Pending → Submitted → Approved → Paid)
  • Export & Submit: Export claims and manually update status after submission to providers
