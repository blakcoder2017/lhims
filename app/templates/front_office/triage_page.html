Understanding the Workflow

Appointment Step 2

A scheduled visit (walk-in or future). Creates a queue entry for the patient.

Check-In Step 5

After vitals, checking in creates an appointment (if needed) and adds patient to doctor's queue. Makes patient visible to doctors.

Encounter Step 6

Created by doctor when seeing patient. Documents clinical findings, orders tests, prescribes medications.

Current Workflow:
  1. Registration → Patient demographics and financial screening
  2. Vitals (You are here) → Record vital signs
  3. Check-In → Creates appointment automatically and adds to doctor's queue
  4. Doctor Creates Encounter → When doctor sees patient, they create the clinical encounter

What is Check-In?

Check-In creates an appointment (if one doesn't exist) and adds the patient to the doctor's queue. Once checked in, the patient becomes visible to doctors who can then create a clinical encounter.

Note: This is different from creating an encounter. The doctor will create the encounter when they see the patient.
