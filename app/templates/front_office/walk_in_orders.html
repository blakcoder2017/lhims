Walk-in Services & Payment Flow
  1. Department / Pharmacy Staff: Search for the patient, capture the service (lab, radiology, pharmacy sale, procedure) and submit.
  2. System: Automatically creates an invoice & charge. Print/download invoice for the patient.
  3. Front Desk: Collect payment, print receipt, then click Check In to notify the department that payment is confirmed.
  4. Department: Receives the receipt and delivers the service or dispenses medication.

Reminder: Orders can only be checked in after the invoice balance is zero (payment confirmed).
