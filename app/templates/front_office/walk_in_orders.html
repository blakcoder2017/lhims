Walk-in Orders Workflow

For walk-in patients who need lab tests, radiology studies, or procedures:

  1. Search and select the patient
  2. Select the service from the dropdown (or type manually if not in list)
  3. Fill in additional details and click "Create Order"
  4. Once created, click "Check In" to mark the order as ready for processing
